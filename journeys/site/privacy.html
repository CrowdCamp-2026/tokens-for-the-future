What happens to your details

Crowd Work Journeys is a small tool built at CrowdCamp 2026 for HCOMP + CI 2026. This page says what it collects when you sign in and post notes, and what you can do about it.

  • Your details stay with the CrowdCamp team. Nothing you give us is sold or handed to sponsors, conference organizers or anyone else.
  • Other attendees see only a pseudonym. You get one at sign-up, like “Calibrated Starling”, and can change it. Your name, affiliation and email or phone number are seen by the CrowdCamp team only.

Who is responsible

The CrowdCamp 2026 team that runs Crowd Work Journeys. Questions and requests: [organizer contact to add before launch].

The site and its database are hosted by Cloudflare.

What this is for

The tool plans a route through the conference around one of thirteen questions and collects attendees’ notes on the talks: comments, questions, criticisms, how each talk relates to the question, and when people think they will live in that future.

The CrowdCamp team may analyze the notes and quote them in a report or paper about the future of crowd work. Quotes are published without names or affiliations.

What is collected

  • Your name and affiliation. Seen by the CrowdCamp team only. Other attendees see your pseudonym instead.
  • Your pseudonym, chosen at random when you sign up. You can change it in “Your details”. Shown next to the notes you post, including earlier ones when you change it.
  • Your email address or phone number. Used to sign you back in, and to contact you if you ticked the follow-up box. Never shown on the site. Within the team, only the lead organizer can see it.
  • Your notes and answers: the text you write, the kind of note, how the talk relates to your topic, and your answer on timing.
  • A keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself. It limits how many notes arrive from one network in a few minutes and cannot be turned back into an address.
  • Your country as reported by the network, used to preselect the phone country code.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to five days, and a pass cookie that remembers you opened the participant link. Both are strictly necessary and do no tracking.
  • On your device only: the talks you star. This stays in your browser and never reaches us.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no social-media buttons. The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it serves them.

Please do not write personal details about other people in a note. A note is about a talk and its ideas.

Your choices

  • Erase your details yourself, at any time, from “Your details” once signed in. Your name, affiliation, pseudonym and contact are deleted and cannot be recovered. Your notes stay on the site as “Anonymous”.
  • Sign out on a shared device from the same page.
  • Ask the team to remove a note, or anything else, at the contact above.

How long it is kept

Names, affiliations and contacts are deleted by 31 March 2027. Notes are kept after that without names, for the report or paper.
